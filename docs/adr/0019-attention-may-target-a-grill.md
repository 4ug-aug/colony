# Attention may target a Grill

Attention is the workspace's durable “come back here” signal for an Account.
Inviting someone to an invite-only Grill creates Attention aimed at that Grill,
just as room mentions and terminal runs create Attention aimed at a Room. We
rejected a separate Grill notification type and rejected routing Grill invites
only through Room messages: both would duplicate Attention or force ephemeral
Grills to depend on durable Rooms.
